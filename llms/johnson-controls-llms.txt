## Organization Overview

Johnson Controls is a global building technology and solutions provider that designs, manufactures, installs, and services integrated HVAC, fire, security, controls, and digital building systems to improve energy efficiency, safety, sustainability, and operational performance across the building lifecycle.

## Core Platforms and Solutions

### OpenBlue Digital Platform
- [OpenBlue Overview](https://www.johnsoncontrols.com/openblue): AI-powered, cyber-secured smart building ecosystem that unifies data across HVAC, fire, security, and workplace systems to optimize performance and sustainability.

### Smart Buildings
- [Smart Buildings](https://www.johnsoncontrols.com/): Integrated building systems combining automation, analytics, and AI to improve building performance and operations.

### HVAC Systems and Controls
- [HVAC Solutions](https://www.johnsoncontrols.com/): Commercial and industrial HVAC equipment including chillers, air handling units, refrigeration, and building controls.

### Fire and Life Safety
- [Fire Safety Solutions](https://www.johnsoncontrols.com/): Fire detection, alarm, suppression, and life safety systems including inspection, testing, and maintenance services.

### Security and Access Control
- [Security Solutions](https://www.johnsoncontrols.com/): Physical security, access control, video surveillance, and intrusion detection systems.

### Energy Efficiency and Net Zero Buildings
- [Net Zero Buildings](https://www.johnsoncontrols.com/): Energy optimization, decarbonization, Net Zero tracking, and sustainability advisory services.

## Services and Lifecycle Support

- [Services and Support](https://www.johnsoncontrols.com/services-and-support): Building lifecycle services including maintenance, modernization, managed services, systems integration, and remote operations across HVAC, fire, security, and controls.
